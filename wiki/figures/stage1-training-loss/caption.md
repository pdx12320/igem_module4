Stage I training trajectories. All available logged training-loss summaries are shown for five model variants: transcriptome-adapted LAMAR with all parameters tuned, original LAMAR with all parameters tuned, their frozen-encoder counterparts, and random initialization with all parameters tuned. Each line represents one run; the training split contains 3,835 region records. Logs end at different epochs, and no values are extrapolated beyond them. The adapted run uses the full final-run log through epoch 7; the other variants use their supplied checkpoint histories. Values are logging-window training summaries, not independent replicates. Lines connect observations without smoothing, uncertainty intervals, or significance tests. The configurations and run lengths differ, so the curves alone do not establish the causal benefit of adaptation.
