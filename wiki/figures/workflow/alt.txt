Two-row workflow. The training row links human transcript sequences, continued pretraining, RNA-seq-labelled fine-tuning and stage-specific evaluation with validation and test data. Trained models feed a proposed application row: reference or user-supplied transcriptome, Stage I region classification, Stage II cytidine ranking and ranked candidate sites for experimental validation. A note states that Stage II evaluation uses positive regions, split overlap limits independence and end-to-end screening remains unevaluated.
