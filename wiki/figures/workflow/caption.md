**Module 4 workflow.** Human transcript sequences support continued LAMAR pretraining, followed by fine-tuning with RNA-seq-derived labels and stage-specific evaluation. The proposed application first classifies transcript regions (Stage I), then ranks cytidine candidates within selected regions (Stage II) for experimental validation. Stage II evaluation includes positive regions only. Sequence overlap limits split independence, and the integrated transcriptome-screening pipeline remains unevaluated.
